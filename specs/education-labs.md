# Education labs & classrooms (spec draft)

**Status:** draft  
**Scope:** repeatable lab rebuilds, curriculum app transitions, and safe student/staff profile portability.

## Goals
- Windows lab → Linux lab checklists with app replacement catalogs.
- Student presets for coursework folders/project exports and safe IDE settings guidance.
- Instructor/admin dry-run review before reset/reimage.
- Offline/USB-first workflows for low bandwidth.

## Safety & privacy
- **Student privacy first:** minimize metadata; no unnecessary personal profiling.
- **No cross-user leakage:** shared-machine workflows must isolate users and wipe/export only approved scopes.
- **No secrets:** never transfer passwords/cookies/sessions; use institution identity/sync systems.
- Local-first by default; no cloud dependency.

## Capabilities (planned)
- Lab profile templates (per course/image role).
- Shared-machine “review mode” report: what will be kept/exported/removed (dry-run).
- Accessibility checklist included in every lab transition plan.
- Verifiable export bundles for coursework handoff (checksums).
